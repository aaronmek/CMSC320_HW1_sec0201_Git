Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: We might use Git to track changes made to a project, restore earlier versions, work on separate features through branches, and collaborate with other people without overwriting their work.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to healthcare. For example, hospitals can analyze patient data to predict disease risks, recognize patterns, and help doctors make better treatment decisions.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines the histories of two branches and may create a separate merge commit. Rebasing reapplies commits onto a new starting point, which creates a more linear commit history.
